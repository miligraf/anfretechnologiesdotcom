Olive Farm Sim

A cozy, decision-driven olive farming sim — groves, mill, and the pursuit of Grand Cru oil.

Four groves Eight varieties One mill No ads, ever
On the App Store ↗

Every autumn asks the same question: pick green for a peppery, structured oil — or wait for ripe fruit and a buttery one, and gamble a season's work against the olive fly. Everything else in the year is preparation for the answer.

II.

The Year

Grove to bottle
i.

Grow & Tend

Prune in winter dormancy, guard the spring bloom, irrigate through summer drought, and scout for the olive fly before it takes the crop. Every season offers more work than you can do.

ii.

Press & Filter

Stone mill, centrifuge, or hydraulic press — then Olio Nuovo, paper, or natural settling. Two decisions turn fruit into character, and the right pairing turns character into grade.

iii.

Sell & Build

Six buyer channels, gated by quality and reputation — from the bulk truck to gourmet retailers, premium chefs, and two region-exclusive elite houses. Your oil is only as good as your worst choice.

Colophon

Support & correspondence

Olive Farm Sim is a single-player game — no account, no timers, no ads, fully playable offline. Two groves are free; Tuscany, Tunisia, and the Estate Bundle are optional purchases. For help, feedback, or anything else, write to the studio.
