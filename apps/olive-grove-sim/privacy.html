Privacy Policy

How Olive Farm Sim handles your information.

Last updated · Jul 23, 2026 Plain language
English Deutsch Français Italiano Español Português
i.

Overview

Olive Farm Sim is a single-player simulation game. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses, stores, and transmits.

The app contains no advertising of any kind, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

ii.

Data collection

The app does not require an account, login, or registration to play. It does not collect your name, email address, or other personally identifiable information.

Third-party services integrated into the app may collect limited, pseudonymous data as described below.

  • In-App Purchases (RevenueCat) — the app uses RevenueCat to manage optional in-app purchases. RevenueCat may process anonymous device identifiers and purchase transaction data to deliver and restore purchases. No personal information is shared. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Analytics (Firebase / Google Analytics) — the app records anonymous, app-instance-scoped usage events (such as starting a game or completing a harvest) and aggregate properties to understand how the game is played. This data is pseudonymous — it is not tied to a login or your real identity — and Google may derive an approximate location from your IP address. See Firebase Privacy & Security and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Crash diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics) — when the app crashes, it sends crash logs, device and diagnostic information, and an install identifier to Google. This helps us fix bugs and is not used to identify you. See Firebase Privacy & Security.
iii.

No advertising, no tracking

Olive Farm Sim shows no ads and includes no advertising SDK. The app does not access the iOS Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt (because it never tracks), and does not share data with data brokers or advertising networks.

iv.

Local storage

Game save data — your grove's progress across up to three estate slots, settings, and achievements — is stored locally on your device using standard platform storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

v.

Network usage

The app makes network requests to verify in-app purchases (RevenueCat) and to send anonymous analytics and crash diagnostics to Firebase (Google). The app may also load fonts via Google Fonts on some platforms; see the Google Fonts Privacy FAQ.

Core gameplay is fully offline and does not require an internet connection.

vi.

In-app purchases

The game is free to play with two of its four regions included. Optional one-time purchases unlock the remaining regions (individually or as the Estate Bundle) and a cosmetic supporter pack. There are no subscriptions, no consumable currencies, and no ads to remove. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. A Restore Purchases option is available in Settings.

vii.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information — such as a name or email address — from anyone, including children under 13. The analytics identifiers described above are pseudonymous and are not used to identify individuals. The app contains no ads and is suitable for all ages.

viii.

Third-party services

Olive Farm Sim integrates the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management. Privacy policy.
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — anonymous usage analytics. Privacy & Security.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash reporting. Privacy & Security.
  • Google Fonts — typography. Privacy FAQ.
ix.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
